Questions, answered

Is Still really free?

Yes — free forever. No account, no trial period, no locked features, no "free during beta" bait. Confirm your email and you get the plugin. We make our money on our paid plugins; Still is our hello.

What noise does it remove?

Steady background noise: preamp hiss, room tone, AC and fan rumble, electrical hum. For sudden one-off sounds (coughs, chair squeaks) you'll still want an editor — that's a different job.

Mac? Windows? Formats?

Right now: macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon + Intel, signed and notarized), VST3 / AU / AAX — yes, it loads in Pro Tools. Windows is in the works; you'll get an email the day it ships.

What's new in v1.0.1?

July 2026 — fixed a bug where suppression could pull down continuous, busy material instead of just the noise (loud audio can now never be attenuated, by design), plus stereo-path hardening. Free updates, forever — that's the deal.
