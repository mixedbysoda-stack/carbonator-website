01 / The dial

Why one dial beats a rack of sliders

Adaptive — no learn step

Still tracks your noise floor continuously across 8 frequency bands. No noise-profile capture, no "hold quiet for 5 seconds." Drop it in and turn the dial.

No underwater artifacts

Still works in the time domain — not FFT — so the watery, chirpy artifacts of typical noise removers are impossible by design. Its worst case is gentle, musical softening.

Zero latency

0 samples of delay. Track through it, monitor through it, mix with it — no timing smear, no delay compensation drama.
