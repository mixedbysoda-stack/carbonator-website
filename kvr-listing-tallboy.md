## Product Description
(The KVR editor has a "Source" button if you want to paste HTML. The text below is
plain, so pasting it straight into the WYSIWYG works fine. Bold the three cartridge
headings after pasting if you want them to stand out.)

TALLBOY listens to the pitch of whatever you feed it and replays that performance
through a four-channel console sound chip. It is not a filter sitting over the top of
your audio. It is an actual chip voice following your line.

Three cartridges run in series, and each one has its own power LED on its bay card.
Click it to insert or eject that stage. Any combination works, including none, in which
case TALLBOY passes audio through completely untouched.

CHIP 4CH RESYNTH
Tracks the pitch and envelope of your input, then rebuilds it on four hardware-modelled
channels.
- Two pulse channels, four duty cycles
- 32-sample, 4-bit wavetable
- 15/7-bit LFSR noise, transient-keyed
- 11-bit frequency register detune

ARP + GLIDE SEQUENCER
Shapes the chip voice into a pattern. Locks to your project tempo, or free-runs with
swing.
- UP, DOWN, UPDN, RAND, CHRD
- 1/4 to 1/32, host-synced
- One to four octaves
- Portamento up to 400 ms

CRUSH ROM DECIMATOR
Bit and sample-rate reduction with deliberate aliasing. Works on anything, chip voice
or not.
- 1 to 16 bits
- Rate divide 1-64, with jitter
- Drive into the quantiser
- Post filter, 18 kHz down to 540 Hz

There are no knobs. You drive TALLBOY with a D-pad and a four-shade dot-matrix display,
six parameters per cartridge with a live value bar. SELECT swaps the cartridge page,
START is a true bypass that nulls perfectly against dry, and A/B fullscreens the
visualiser so you can watch the four channels, the arp steps, or the crushed waveform.

Best on vocals and vocal doubles, bass lines, single-note leads and hooks, and
monophonic synth parts. It is also good for transitions and risers, turning a hook into
a counter-melody, chiptune layers under a real instrument, and game or video soundtrack
work. For drums and full mixes, eject the resynth and arp cartridges and run CRUSH ROM
on its own as a straight decimator.

$20 one-time. No subscription, no iLok, no dongle, no account. Three machines on one
licence, and it works offline after a single activation. Lifetime updates.

A free demo is available with no email required. It is identical to the paid version
except audio plays for 60 seconds then mutes for 10, on a loop. The demo is macOS VST3
and AU only.
