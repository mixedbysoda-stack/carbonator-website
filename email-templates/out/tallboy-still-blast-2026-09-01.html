TALLBOY turns your audio into a chip performance

You grabbed Still to get noise out of your recordings. TALLBOY sits at the opposite end of the chain - it takes a clean signal and turns it into something new.

Feed it a vocal, a guitar line, a synth lead - anything with a pitch. It tracks the note and the envelope in real time and replays the performance through a four-channel console sound chip. Not a bitcrusher pasted on top - an actual chip voice following your line.

The demo is free and does not ask for an email. Load it on a vocal you already have and you will know in thirty seconds whether it belongs in your rack.
