The Plugin
  • Cola: Warm, punchy console-style saturation. Great on drums and mix bus.
  • Cherry: Aggressive tube-style grit. Perfect for bass and guitars.
  • Grape: Lo-fi, vintage character. Amazing on vocals and synths.
  • Lemon-Lime: Bright, airy harmonic saturation. Ideal for adding presence.
  • Orange Cream: Smooth, creamy tape-style warmth. Works on everything.

Carbonated mode blends all 5 saturation circuits together for a unique, complex harmonic character. It's our signature sound.

The Fizz knob controls the entire signal chain for the selected flavor — drive, compression, EQ, and makeup gain all morph together as you turn it up. One knob, full control.
