About Carbonator

Carbonator is an analog saturation plugin by Carbonated Audio featuring five circuit-modeled saturation flavors: Cola, Cherry, Grape, Lemon-Lime, and Orange Cream. Each flavor delivers a distinct harmonic character — from subtle warmth to aggressive drive — controlled by a single Fizz knob.

Priced at $20 one-time (no subscription), Carbonator includes lifetime updates and runs on every major DAW. Available as VST3, AU, AAX, and Standalone on macOS (including Apple Silicon) and Windows.
