SUBJECT: Getting the most out of Still

BODY:

Hey,

You picked up Still a couple of days ago - wanted to send one practical note
rather than a sales email.

The mistake most people make with it is setting the dial while the performer
is talking. Do it the other way around. Find a gap in the take where it is
just room tone, push the dial up until the noise floor drops out, then ease
back until it stops sounding closed-in. That setting will hold for the whole
take.

It is at its best on the constant stuff - preamp hiss, fan noise, air
conditioning, room tone. It is not a gate and it is not trying to be, so
intermittent noise like traffic or a chair creak is still a manual fix.

Zero latency, so you can leave it in while you track.

If it turns out to be useful I would genuinely like to know what you put it
on. And if it does something strange on your material, tell me that too -
reports from actual sessions are how this thing improves.
