Installation

1
macOS: Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts. Choose which formats to install (VST3, AU, AAX, Standalone).
2
Windows: Extract the .zip and copy the VST3 plugin to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
3
Rescan plugins in your DAW (FL Studio: Options → Manage plugins → Start scan). Carbonator will appear under Effects → Filter.
4
Drop it on a track and start tweaking. Pick a flavor, turn the FIZZ knob, and toggle Carbonated mode.
