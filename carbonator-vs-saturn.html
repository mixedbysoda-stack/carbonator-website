When to Choose Carbonator

  • You want fast, musical saturation without menu-diving
  • You work on multiple tracks and need different character per channel — that's what the 5 flavors are for
  • You're a beatmaker or producer who values creative flow over surgical precision
  • Budget matters — $20 vs $154 lets you spend the difference on other tools
  • You want light CPU usage so you can stack instances across your session
  • You produce hip-hop, lo-fi, pop, or electronic music where vibe matters more than precision

When to Choose Saturn 2

  • You need multiband saturation — processing lows, mids, and highs independently
  • You're a mastering engineer or mix engineer who needs surgical distortion control
  • You want modulation on your saturation parameters (LFOs, envelopes, etc.)
  • You need 16+ distortion algorithms for maximum variety per band
  • You enjoy deep sound design and don't mind the learning curve
  • You already own other FabFilter plugins and want consistency in your workflow

The Bottom Line

Saturn 2 is a masterclass in multiband distortion — if you need per-band control with modulation, nothing beats it. But for most producers, that level of complexity slows down the creative process.


Carbonator takes the opposite approach: maximum character with minimum friction. Five flavors that each sound genuinely different, one knob to control intensity, $20. Many producers keep both — Saturn for surgical work, Carbonator for fast creative decisions.
