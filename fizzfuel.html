Frequently Asked

FIZZFUEL is a multi-effect plugin built around a manual gearshift. You throw the stick into a gear to pick Drive, Reverb, Delay, Pitch, Filter, or R for a clean reference, then shape it with the clutch (intensity) and a style selector. It started life as a saturation gearbox and grew into a full effects rack.
Throw the shifter into the gear you want, exactly like a real gearbox. Each of the six positions is a different effect, and switching crossfades smoothly so there are no clicks — reverb and delay tails ring out as you change gears.
