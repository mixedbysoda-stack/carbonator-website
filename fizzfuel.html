FAQ

Frequently asked.

What is FIZZFUEL?
FIZZFUEL is a multi-effect plugin built around a manual gearshift. You throw the stick into a gear to pick one of six effects — Drive, Reverb, Delay, Pitch, Filter, or a clean bypass — and shape it with the clutch (intensity) and a style selector. It started life as a saturation gearbox and grew into a full effects rack.
How do you switch between effects?
Throw the shifter into the gear you want, exactly like a real gearbox. Each of the six positions is a different effect, and switching crossfades smoothly so there are no clicks — reverb and delay tails ring out as you change gears.
What effects and styles are included?
Drive (Tape/Tube/Console/Clip/Fuzz), Reverb (Room/Hall/Plate/Canyon), Delay (Slap/1-8/Dotted/Long), Pitch (-Oct/+5th/Detune/Shimmer), and Filter (Telephone/LP Sweep/Tilt/Reso) — over twenty styles in all, plus a clean A/B gear.
Is the delay tempo-synced?
Yes. The Delay gear locks to your DAW's tempo — slap, eighth, dotted-eighth, and long divisions — with filtered, self-oscillating feedback on the clutch.
What formats are supported?
FIZZFUEL is available as VST3, AU, and AAX on macOS (Intel + Apple Silicon, universal, Pro Tools–signed), and as VST3 on Windows (64-bit) with an easy auto-installer.
How many computers can I use it on?
Up to 3 machines with one license. To move an activation, deactivate on the old machine and activate on the new one.
